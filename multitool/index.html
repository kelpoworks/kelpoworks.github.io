what it does

  • Model

    Edit vertices, edges and faces. Extrude, inset, loop cut, weld and mirror as you go. Start from a cube, plane, sphere or cylinder.

  • Sculpt

    A practical set of core brushes for shaping the mesh and adding detail by hand.

  • Remesh

    Simplify or remesh when a mesh gets heavy. Mask the details you want to keep and compare before you apply.

  • UV

    Automatic unwrapping without overlaps, or box and triplanar projection. Check the layout and texel density before painting.

  • Paint

    Paint color, metal, smoothness and height right on the surface, in layers. Start from scratch or paint over existing textures. Characters too, in their current pose.

  • Colliders & export

    Quick colliders and simplified mesh colliders. When it is done, export the mesh, textures, material and prefab.

good to know

The tools are basic on purpose. Here is what that means in practice.

good for

  • Blockouts, prototypes and greyboxing
  • Props, modular pieces and level geometry
  • Fixing up and repainting meshes you already have
  • Painting characters in their current pose
  • Quick colliders and simple collision meshes

not for

  • Replacing Blender or other full 3D suites
  • Booleans or hand-made retopology
  • Detailed UV editing by hand
  • Reshaping skinned meshes (painting them works)

the small print
